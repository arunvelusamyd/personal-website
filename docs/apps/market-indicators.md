---

**September 2026 Update:** Positioning and market internals are pulling in opposite directions. BofA's August survey put fund manager cash at 3.5%, among the lowest readings since the survey began and a firm contrarian sell — yet the market underneath has thinned out. Breadth fell from ~73% to roughly 63% of S&P 500 names above their 200-day average, back below the healthy threshold, and the Fear & Greed Index round-tripped from 65 (Greed) to 44 (Fear) in a fortnight. AAII bulls slid further to 32.9%. Valuations eased only slightly, CAPE to 41.74 and the Buffett Indicator to 238%. Credit refuses to corroborate any of the anxiety: high-yield spreads tightened to 263 bps and OFR stress fell to -3.03. The macro read is unresolved — GDPNow has Q3 tracking at 4.8%, an early and volatile estimate, set against a July payrolls print that fell 23,000 and labor and inflation data that have not refreshed since.
