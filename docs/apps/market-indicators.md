---

**September 2026 Update:** Market internals and the economy have decoupled. The S&P 500 slipped about 1% over the first half of the month, but underneath it the tape thinned further — roughly 57% of S&P 500 names still sit above their 200-day average, and CNN's Fear & Greed Index slid from 44 to 28, deep into Fear. The macro data went the other way: August payrolls added 162,000 after July's meagre 21,000, GDPNow has Q3 tracking at 4.4%, and the 12-month rolling deficit narrowed to 5.5% of GDP. The cost shows up in prices, with headline inflation firming to 3.4% and the Misery Index ticking to 7.5 — comfortable, but moving the wrong way for anyone counting on rate relief. Positioning finally loosened a little: BofA's September survey lifted fund manager cash to 3.9%, still inside contrarian sell territory, and AAII bulls rebounded to 38.0%. Credit never flinched — high yield at 271 bps, OFR stress at -2.46.
